# Chickososik

## What it is

“Chickososik” is a route-building puzzle game for players who enjoy connecting paths and solving boards in a low number of moves. Turn path tiles, then send one to three chicks along the routes. A trail is complete when every chick reaches a coop.

## Launch and onboarding

1. On first launch, the game opens directly to “Chickososik” with the “Trail atlas” button and the first puzzle, “Sunlit Lane”, under the banner “CHALK DOWNS · TRAIL 01”. The board shows a chick, a red coop, two gold pivots, “1 chick” and “0 turns”.
2. The instruction below the board reads “Tap a gold pivot to turn it. Join the chick to the red coop.” The controls are “Release chicks”, “Undo”, “Hint” and “Reset”. “Undo” and “Reset” start disabled. There is no separate onboarding screen or confirmation to dismiss.
3. Tap the first gold pivot to the right of the chick once, then tap “Release chicks”. The button reads “On the trail…” while the chick walks. A successful first attempt shows “Everyone is home!” and “Best on this trail: 1 turns.” Tap “Next trail” to open “Willow Fork”.

## Screens

### “Chickososik” trail board

The screen shows the “Chickososik” masthead, the “Trail atlas” button, an illustrated banner with the region and two-digit trail number, and the current trail title. The scrollable board shows the chick count, turn count, starting chicks, paths, coops and scenery. The count reads “1 chick”, “2 chicks” or “3 chicks”, alongside a readout such as “0 turns”.

- “Trail atlas” opens the atlas at the current trail’s region.
- Gold-pivot tiles have no printed labels. Tap a straight or bend to turn it, or tap a fork to switch between its two directions. Each tap adds one turn. Grass, starts, coops, merges, stumps and bridges are fixed.
- “Release chicks” runs the current routes. Chicks walk one at a time, leaving dotted tracks. Chicks that reach a coop appear beside it. During the run the button reads “On the trail…”.
- “Undo” restores the board and turn count from before the last tile change or reset on the current visit.
- “Hint” outlines one tile in yellow and displays its coordinates, for example “Try turning row 2, column 2.” It leaves the tiles and turn count as they are.
- “Reset” restores the current trail’s starting arrangement and “0 turns”. The atlas still records any earlier completion and best result for that trail.
- “Next trail” appears after a successful walk while unfinished trails remain. It opens the next unfinished trail in number order, wrapping to earlier numbers if needed.
- “Explore the atlas” appears after a successful walk when all 24 trails are complete. It opens “Trail atlas”.

VoiceOver identifies each tile by row, column and type, for example “Row 2, column 2, straight”. The type names are “grass”, “start”, “coop”, “straight”, “bend”, “fork”, “merge”, “stump” and “bridge”. Rotatable tiles also announce their open directions using “north”, “east”, “south” and “west”.

### “Trail atlas”

A scrollable sheet opens with the title “Trail atlas”. It shows the region selectors, a landscape illustration and completion count for the selected region, and eight numbered trail cards. Each card shows a miniature of its current board. The active trail is highlighted. A checkmark seal marks completed trails.

- The × button, announced as “Close atlas”, closes the sheet and returns to the current board. Swiping the sheet down also dismisses it.
- “Chalk Downs”, “Willow Mill” and “Lantern Orchard” change the cards and completion count shown in the sheet. In a compact layout they read “Downs”, “Mill” and “Orchard”. VoiceOver uses the full region names.
- Tap any numbered trail card to open that board and close the atlas. All 24 trails are available from first launch. Completed trails can be replayed.
- A fresh region shows “0 OF 8 TRAILS HOME”. The count rises with each first completion in that region. VoiceOver labels a card with its number, title and status, for example “Trail 1, Sunlit Lane, open” or “Trail 1, Sunlit Lane, complete”.

## Features

- “Trail atlas” with 24 replayable trails, eight each in “Chalk Downs”, “Willow Mill” and “Lantern Orchard”. Cards preview the current board and show completion status.
- Gold and brass pivots for turning straight paths and bends and switching forks. Some trails bring lanes together at a junction or cross them at a timber bridge, where each chick carries straight on.
- “Release chicks” animates the walk, traces the routes and marks the tile where a route stops.
- “Undo”, “Hint” and “Reset” help players revise or restart an attempt.
- A running turn count, “Best on this trail: [count] turns.”, and completion messages for a trail, a region and the atlas.
- VoiceOver labels for the atlas and board, layouts that adapt to larger text, and shorter walk animations with Reduce Motion enabled.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- “Release chicks” works on an unfinished board. The walk stops at the first blocked route, and the remaining chicks may stay at their starts. “The trail is blocked.” appears with “Start at the marked tile and check the two neighbouring paths.” Check the outlined tile and adjacent paths, turn the necessary pivots, and tap “Release chicks” again. Routes must enter each coop from the left.
- While “On the trail…” is showing, the main button, pivots, “Undo”, “Hint” and “Reset” are disabled. Wait for the walk to end. “Trail atlas” remains available, and selecting a trail ends the current walk.
- Fixed tiles do nothing when tapped. Look for a gold pivot on a straight, bend or fork to change the route.
- “Undo” starts disabled and becomes available after a turn or reset. Selecting a trail or relaunching clears the undo history while retaining the saved board and turn count.
- “Hint” highlights one tile at a time. Tapping it again before changing that tile can repeat the same instruction. Turn the highlighted tile and use “Hint” again if needed. When the board matches the solution, “Hint” is disabled. Tap “Release chicks” to register the completion.
- “Reset” is disabled when the board is in its starting arrangement with “0 turns”. Once a tile has been turned, “Reset” restores the starting board and zeroes the attempt counter. Tap “Undo” to recover the pre-reset attempt during the same visit.
- “Next trail” skips completed trails and can wrap back to an earlier unfinished trail. To pick a specific trail, open “Trail atlas”. Once all 24 are complete, a successful walk shows “Explore the atlas” in place of “Next trail”.
- “0 OF 8 TRAILS HOME” is the starting progress count for each region. The eight cards are already available. Completing a trail for the first time increases its region’s count. Replays and resets keep past completions on record.
- Reopening a trail restores its board, including a solved arrangement. To see the success message or best count again, tap “Release chicks”. To replay from the starting arrangement, tap “Reset” when enabled.

## Starter content and resume

All 24 built-in trails are present from the start, with their starting tile arrangements and instructions. The first launch opens “Sunlit Lane” with zero turns and no completed trails. There is no separate sample save.

The app saves the selected trail, tile positions and turn counts for each trail, completed trails and best turn counts on the device. Unfinished boards can be resumed after switching trails or relaunching. Walk animation, hint highlights, current result messages and undo history are temporary.

## Data and support

Game progress stays on this device. The game can be played offline and has no on-screen “Contact” or “Support” control.

None.

## Platform

iPhone and iPad, with a minimum of iOS 17.0. The build declares portrait, upside-down portrait and both landscape orientations. Wide layouts can place the board beside the title, instructions and controls. Larger accessibility text sizes use a stacked layout.

The app ships with English (UK), Arabic and Traditional Chinese interface text. It follows the system’s app language selection and uses English (UK) as the fallback. Arabic uses Arabic-formatted numerals and right-to-left interface layout while the board and its atlas previews retain left-to-right orientation. All 24 trails are available across regions.
